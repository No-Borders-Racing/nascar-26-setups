General tuning tips

Tight means the front won't turn (understeer). Loose means the rear steps out (oversteer). Change one thing at a time, in small steps, and compare over the same number of laps.

Per corner
SettingLowerHigher
Spring rateSofter, more grip over bumps, more body roll.Stiffer, quicker response, less roll.
Bump (compression)Softer. The corner is pushed up more easily, so it absorbs bumps better and takes load more gradually.Firmer. The corner resists being pushed up, so load reaches the chassis more sharply and the car feels stiffer over bumps.
ReboundThe shock extends quickly. The tire follows the track better, but the car can feel bouncy or float.The shock extends slowly. The corner stays loaded longer, but the car can pack down and feel harsh over bumps.
Tire pressure (psi)More grip, more heat and wear.Less grip, less drag, a bit faster on straights, harder ride.
CamberFlatter tire, even wear, less cornering grip.More cornering grip up to a point, then an edge overheats. Less straight-line grip.

These are general stock car rules, not tested in NASCAR 26. Confirm each change in the game. Wheel lock and steering offset are the two I'm least able to confirm.
